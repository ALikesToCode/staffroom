Staffroom: measuring the on-device check

A developer page. With nothing added to the address it runs 24 fixed, invented cases through the on-device model's part of the check: 12 where the advice conflicts with a withheld fact and 12 where it does not. Each case runs once; add ?runs=3 to run each three times. Nothing is sent anywhere: the route is ONLY_ON_DEVICE. In these single-fact sets the model's own answer is counted: its "no" is shown as "conflict" here, though the app shows it to the teacher as a doubt and never strikes an item on the model's word alone.

?set=rules: the rule layer on its own, 62 fresh pairs of a step and a confirmed constraint. No model is used, so it runs in any browser. A case the rules get wrong stays in the set and is counted.

?set=full: the whole sample note, a vault built from fixed entries, the constraints the rules propose (all confirmed) and six fixed drafts of five three-step items each, of which one or two in each draft have a step that a withheld fact rules out. Each row shows the two layers together, and each on its own.

?set=heldout: 16 single-fact cases the on-device model has not been tuned on, for one run.

One fact at a time is the default, as in the app; add perFact=0 to ask about all of a child's facts in one prompt. For example eval.html?set=full&runs=3.
